## 🔒 Implemented Requirements & Technical Controls

### 1. The 4 Data Tiers ([`server/services/dataMinimiser.ts`](file:///d:/TRACK-2-03-PRIVACY-PRESERVING-CONSUMER-INTELLIGENCE-FOR-RESPONSIBLE-PERSONALISATION/server/services/dataMinimiser.ts))
Data only moves **right** (towards less detail), never back:
- **T0 &bull; PROHIBITED (Never collected, never inferred)**: Religion, caste, health condition, pregnancy, sexual life, children's data, location tracking, credit/khata balance.
- **T1 &bull; RAW (Transient, deleted $\le$ 7 days)**: Name, phone, exact time, item codes, exact amount, payment mode.
- **T2 &bull; MINIMISED (Stored, pseudonymous, needs consent P1)**: 
  - Phone converted to **HMAC-SHA256 keyed-hash** with secret shop key (plain hashing is banned because 10B phone numbers can be reversed).
  - Time rounded to **week number** (AG-6).
  - Sensitive products (OTC health, baby care, puja, tobacco) relabeled to **'Restricted'** (AG-7, PT-01 to PT-03).
  - Amounts mapped into **4 bands** (0–200, 200–500, 500–1000, 1000+).
- **T3 &bull; AGGREGATE (Shown)**: Groups of $k \ge 10$ with Laplace noise.

### 2. Aggregation & Differential Privacy Engine ([`server/services/aggregationEngine.ts`](file:///d:/TRACK-2-03-PRIVACY-PRESERVING-CONSUMER-INTELLIGENCE-FOR-RESPONSIBLE-PERSONALISATION/server/services/aggregationEngine.ts))
Implements rules **AG-1 to AG-8**:
- **AG-1**: Show a group only if noisy size $\ge 10$; otherwise suppress it or merge it into *"Drifting Regulars"*.
- **AG-2**: Add calibrated Laplace noise:
  $$u \sim U(-0.5, 0.5), \quad \text{noise} = -b \cdot \text{sgn}(u) \cdot \ln(1 - 2|u|), \quad b = \frac{1.0}{\epsilon}$$
  Default $\epsilon = 0.5$ for counts, $\epsilon = 0.5$ for spend per weekly report.
- **AG-3**: Each customer's spend capped at ₹2,000 per period before summing.
- **AG-4**: At most 2 dimensions per table; zero drill-down to rows; no free-text customer queries.
- **AG-5**: Complementary suppression (if one cell is suppressed, hide totals that allow subtraction recovery).

---

### 3. The 15 Prohibited Targeting Registry & Dark Pattern Scanner ([`server/data/prohibitedRegistry.ts`](file:///d:/TRACK-2-03-PRIVACY-PRESERVING-CONSUMER-INTELLIGENCE-FOR-RESPONSIBLE-PERSONALISATION/server/data/prohibitedRegistry.ts))
- Automated code enforcement for **PT-01 to PT-15**:
